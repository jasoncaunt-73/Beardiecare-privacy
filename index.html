BeardieCare Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • App Package: com.aistudio.beardiecare.xqmrtv

1. Overview

BeardieCare ("we", "our", or "the app") is a bearded dragon care and husbandry companion app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what data BeardieCare collects, how it is stored and used, and how you can delete your account and data.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your Google user ID (UID), email address, and basic profile information solely to authenticate your account and isolate your private pet records.
  • Pet & Husbandry Records: Information you voluntarily enter, including bearded dragon profiles (name, morph, sex, hatch date, adoption date, UVB bulb replacement date, enclosure notes), feeding and supplement logs, weight/length logs, terrarium temperature and humidity readings, brumation cycles, and care schedules.
  • Profile Photos (Optional): Selected via Android's zero-permission Photo Picker. Only a local device URI reference is stored for display; BeardieCare does not access your broader photo library.
  • Local Preferences & Reminders: Theme mode (Auto/Light/Dark), temperature unit (°F/°C), 7/8-day supplement cycle configuration, and custom care reminders are stored locally on your device.

3. Permissions Used

  • Internet Access: Used for Google Sign-In and syncing your husbandry data with Cloud Firestore.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used only when you enable care, milestone, or custom reminders.
  • Receive Boot Completed (Optional): Used only to restore your enabled care reminder alarms after your device restarts.

BeardieCare does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad external storage permissions.

4. Data Storage, Security & Third-Party Services

Your account and husbandry records are stored in Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (provided by Google LLC) using security rules that restrict read/write access exclusively to your authenticated user ID.

BeardieCare contains no third-party advertising SDKs, does not sell personal data, and does not share your data with data brokers. Exported veterinary PDF reports and growth/milestone images are generated locally on your device and shared only when you explicitly initiate sharing.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You may delete individual dragon profiles or logs at any time inside the app. To permanently delete your BeardieCare account and all associated cloud data:

  • In the App: Sign in, tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner, and choose Delete Account & Data.
  • By Email Request: Email jasoncaunt73@gmail.com from your registered Google account email with the subject BeardieCare Account Deletion Request, and we will verify and remove your account records within 7 days.

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored preferences and scheduled local reminders from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

BeardieCare is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact: jasoncaunt73@gmail.com

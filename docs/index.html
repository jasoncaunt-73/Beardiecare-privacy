Smart Condition Toggles

Only check basking temperature and UVB on quick morning sweeps? Toggle off Cool Side or Humidity right on the Habitat screen—BeardieCare hides those fields and won’t store dummy values, while Vet PDFs cleanly omit unrecorded metrics. Logged humidity values display clearly as % Humidity.

Brumation Mode Safeguards

Step-by-Step Manual

How to Use BeardieCare

Filter by section or search any feature—from setting up your 8-day supplement plan to exporting a JSON backup.

Sanctuary & Dragons

1. Adding, Editing & Switching Dragons

Set up multiple dragon profiles and customize photos with zero storage permissions.

  1. Sign in with Google, then tap the + button in the top-right bar to create a profile (name, morph, sex, hatch date, Gotcha Day, UVB date, notes).
  2. Tap the dragon’s name in the top-left bar (or Avatar Menu → Manage Dragons) to switch dragons or delete a profile (confirmation required).
  3. Tap the pencil icon on the Sanctuary hero banner to change photos via Android’s zero-permission Photo Picker or update enclosure notes.
Sanctuary & Dragons

2. Live Age Ticker & Milestone Countdowns

Follow exact chronological age and upcoming annual anniversaries.

  1. View exact days old, weeks & days, and the live HH:MM:SS counter on the Sanctuary dashboard.
  2. Life stage pills update automatically: Hatchling (<3m), Juvenile (3–9m), Sub-Adult (9–18m), Adult (18m–7y), Senior (7y+).
  3. Hatchday and Gotcha Day cards count down to your dragon’s next celebration and highlight on the day itself.
Meals & Supplements

3. Logging Meals & Saved Presets

Record greens, feeders, appetite, and calcium/multivitamin dusting in seconds.

  1. Tap + Meal on Sanctuary or open Care → Feedings → Log Meal.
  2. Save favorite combinations via Add Preset for 1-tap logging later (tap × on a preset chip to delete with confirmation).
  3. Supplement chips show full supplement names and pre-select today’s scheduled supplement—or adjust per meal before saving.
Meals & Supplements

4. 7-Day vs 8-Day Supplement Rotation

Tailor weekly or 8-day rolling schedules and assign brand names.

  1. Open Settings → Supplement Rotation Cycle → Rename Supplements to customize names for Calcium + D3, Calcium (No D3), and Multivitamin.
  2. Tap Preview & Amend Schedule (or visit Care → Reminders) to switch between 7-Day (Mon–Sun) and 8-Day (Day 1–8) modes.
  3. In 8-day rolling mode, tap Set Today as Day 1 anytime to realign the cycle to today.
Growth & Reports

5. Weight Charts & Range Filters

Inspect historical weigh-ins, optional body length, and growth velocity.

  1. Tap + Weight on Sanctuary or Growth → Weigh to log weight in grams and optional length in cm.
  2. Once 2+ measurements are saved, tap directly on the curve to inspect any entry’s exact weight, date, and length.
  3. Filter across 1M, 3M, 1Y, or All, and tap any row’s trash icon (with confirmation) to delete an entry.
Growth & Reports

6. Veterinary PDF Reports & Social Cards

Bring organized clinical history to exotic vet visits or share milestones.

  1. Tap Vet Report on Sanctuary or Growth to generate a clean single-page PDF with dragon age, habitat averages, weights, feedings, and brumations.
  2. If Cool Side or Humidity toggles were off for certain checks, unrecorded fields are omitted cleanly rather than showing blanks or fake zeros.
  3. Use Share Card (1080×1080 milestone PNG) or Share Chart (high-res growth PNG) to share to any app.
Habitat & UVB

7. Habitat Temps, % Humidity & Toggles

Track thermal zones cleanly without logging metrics you didn’t measure.

  1. Tap + Climate on Sanctuary or Habitat → Check to record basking temp and UVB bulb status.
  2. Use the Show: Cool Side and Humidity chips on Habitat to toggle either metric. Hidden metrics are not saved in new readings.
  3. Switch between °F and °C in Settings → Temperature Units; existing readings convert automatically and prompt before deletion.
Habitat & UVB

8. T5 HO UVB Bulb 180-Day Guard

Prevent metabolic bone disease by replacing UV tubes before output fades.

  1. Linear T5 HO UVB fluorescent tubes still glow visibly after their UV output drops past ~180 days.
  2. Watch the UVB lifespan bar on the Habitat tab, which flags replacement once 180 days elapse.
  3. Install a fresh bulb and tap Replaced to reset the counter to today.
Brumation

9. Brumation Tracker & Hydration Checks

Safely track winter rest, weight stability, and soak check-ins.

  1. Open Care → Brumation and tap Start Brumation Tracker with your dragon’s starting weight.
  2. Log Hydration Check soaks and weights during rest—BeardieCare warns if weight drops ≥ 10%.
  3. Tap Wake Up once basking and normal feeding resume to archive the completed cycle.
Alerts, Lock & Backup

10. Notifications, Themes & Custom Reminders

Personalize visual appearance and schedule reliable daily or one-off alerts.

  1. Set Appearance Theme to Auto, Light, or Dark in Settings.
  2. Configure your daily feeding alert time and Hatchday/Gotcha Day alerts in Care → Reminders.
  3. Use Settings → Custom Care Reminders → Add Reminder for misting, lights off, or soaks, and verify delivery with Send Test Notification.
Alerts, Lock & Backup

11. App PIN Lock & Biometric Unlock

Protect sensitive logs with a salted PIN and fingerprint or face unlock.

  1. Open Settings → App Lock & Biometrics and toggle PIN Lock to set a 4–6 digit PIN (salted & hashed on-device).
  2. Optionally enable Biometric Unlock to verify via your device’s fingerprint or face prompt.
  3. BeardieCare automatically locks on cold launch and after spending 10+ seconds in the background.
Alerts, Lock & Backup

12. Backup, Restore, Export & Import (JSON)

Move data across accounts/devices or archive offline snapshots anytime.

  1. Go to Settings → Backup & Restore (or tap Avatar Menu → Backup & Restore).
  2. Tap Export JSON to save a .json file via Android’s file picker, or Share Backup to send it directly.
  3. Tap Import / Restore Backup to load any .json snapshot and pick Merge & Add (default, idempotent) or Replace All Data. App PIN & Sign-In are preserved.

Zero-Trust Privacy & Portability

Your Data Stays Yours

BeardieCare authenticates with Google via Credential Manager and isolates every dragon profile, feeding, weigh-in, climate reading, brumation log, and schedule under your private user ID in Cloud Firestore.

  • No ads, no analytics trackers, no data sales.
  • Confirmed Deletions: Every delete button (climates, meals, weights, presets, reminders, dragons, account) asks for confirmation first.
  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: Uses Android’s Photo Picker and Storage Access Framework for JSON backups, PDFs, and share cards.
